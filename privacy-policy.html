Overview

Lumina Isles is a one-time-purchase, fully offline puzzle game. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no login. You buy it once and own it forever.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the game handles — and confirms that we collect no personal data at all.

Offline by design · no network requests

The game is a completely offline, single-player experience. After it is downloaded and installed, it makes no network requests of any kind — it does not connect to the internet to load levels, check licenses, report statistics, or fetch content.

Because the app never goes online, it cannot transmit anything anywhere: we do not share, sell, or disclose any data to any third party, for any purpose. There is no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter that includes personal data, and no tracking of any kind.

No network requests No data sharing No third-party SDKs

Nothing. Lumina Isles does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. There is no account system, so we hold no name, email address, phone number, or identifier for you.

Cloud save & FAQ

Is there cloud save / cross-device sync?

No. Progress is stored locally on the device only. There is no account and no server, so progress does not transfer between devices or after reinstalling the app.

What happens if I uninstall the game?

All local progress is cleared when the app is uninstalled. To keep your progress, stay on the same device. We recommend finishing a run you care about before switching devices.

Can I back up my progress?

On most platforms, your device's own backup (e.g. system cloud backup of app data) may include local storage. We do not provide a separate backup feature.
